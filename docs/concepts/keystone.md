# Keystone concept: "The typing is hiding a decision."

Visual concept: ICEBERG-BUT-NOT-AN-ICEBERG. A large plain gray shape (visible routine work) rests entirely on one small orange element (the hidden judgment). Three renderings, all `gpt-image-2`, `1536x1024`, `--quality high`, generated via `scripts/gen_image.py`.

## keystone-1.png — arch with orange keystone

**Prompt:**

> Flat, clean editorial vector illustration, Stripe or Linear marketing quality. Pure white background #FFFFFF. A wide, low arch built from many large plain rectangular blocks in light gray #F5F5F7 with thin outlines in line gray #E0E0E0, resting on a simple flat ground line in dark #272B33. At the very top center of the arch, one small wedge-shaped keystone block is the only colored element, in bright RFF Orange #FF5219. The keystone is much smaller than the other blocks and easy to miss at first glance, yet visually it is clearly the piece holding the entire gray arch together; the gray blocks lean into it. Composition sits in the lower two thirds of the frame; the top third is empty white space for a title. Minimal, geometric, generous whitespace, soft flat shading only, no heavy gradients, no photorealism, no texture. No text, no letters, no watermark. No people, faces, robots, brains, lightbulbs, or icebergs.

**Verdict:** Works best — the keystone is a universally legible "remove this and it all falls" symbol, the wedge is visibly smaller than the voussoirs, brand palette is exact, and the top third is clean; its one weakness is that the orange is at the apex, so it reads as "crowning" rather than "hidden beneath."
